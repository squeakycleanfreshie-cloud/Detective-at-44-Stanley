Case 1: The Poisoning of Pierre Dubois
Pierre is a man who lives on Rue d’eglise, a street near Stanley Place. He is 35 years of age. He was found at 4:14pm by his partner, who went out to walk their dog. She reports he was supposed to be at work, and left for work at 8:ooam. She left to walk the dog at around 3:1opm, so he must have returned and been murdered around that one hour window. She reports that he was very hot to touch when she found him. He has a very dry mouth and noticeably dilated pupils. The toxicology report came back, and found Atropine in his system, a drug that is found in eye drops, and medicates things like heart issues. Mr Dubois has no prescription to the drug, neither did his partner.
